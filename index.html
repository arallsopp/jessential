Do this first, so nothing gets lost

Safari can delete saved stories and results from a website that hasn't been opened for a week. Installing Jessential! on the Home Screen avoids this, and lets it open without Wi-Fi.

  1. Tap the Share button at the top of Safari.
  2. Tap Add to Home Screen, then Add.
  3. From now on, open Jessential! from that icon only.

Anything saved in Safari doesn't carry over to the icon, so add stories after you've done this.
